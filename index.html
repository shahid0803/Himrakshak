Engineering boundaries

Implemented capabilities are separated from future work so the demonstration remains technically defensible.

Transparent limitations: this is virtual hardware and software simulation only. It has no physical ESP32, calibrated field sensors, physical LoRa radio, or field validation. A compact Decision Tree is trained on synthetic demonstration scenarios and exported to C++; its metrics are synthetic-label agreement, not real landslide prediction accuracy. Several Wokwi features (tilt-Y, displacement, vibration metrics, groundwater and rainfall history) are derived or seeded simulation proxies, not direct instrument measurements.
